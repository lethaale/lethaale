## About

I'm a Web3 CTO and full-stack engineer building trading and prediction-market products end-to-end — from mobile apps and wallet UX down to smart contracts and backend infrastructure. As a CTO I own the product, not just a ticket: architecture, delivery, and the calls in between. I also teach and mentor on Solidity and Ruby on Rails, so I communicate clearly and leave code others can actually build on. Open to freelance and contract engagements across any stack, with web3 as the sweet spot.

## What I'm building

**[Aura](https://aura.money)** — cross-platform trading app (iOS, Android, desktop) unifying Hyperliquid and Polymarket behind one wallet/login: perps, spot, and prediction markets, with Apple Pay / Google Pay onramps and BTC/ETH/SOL deposits. CTO. → [github.com/AuraDotMoney](https://github.com/AuraDotMoney)

**[Perps.fun](https://perps.fun)** — a launchpad for perpetual futures markets on Hyperliquid, built on the HIP-3 standard, with SEDA oracle integration, crowdfunded market seeding, and creator fee-sharing. CTO. → [github.com/Perps-fun](https://github.com/Perps-fun)
